Method and caveats

Sources

311 rows come from the Shahnama Project collection in the Cambridge Digital Library, read from its public IIIF collection and item metadata. The original Shahnama Project website is no longer functional, and the transfer is a work in progress.

369 rows marked SnP archive come from the original Shahnama Project database, read from 845 archived manuscript record cards in the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine. They cover holdings not yet migrated to CUDL, including all 56 Topkapı Palace Library copies in range, Tehran (Gulistan, Majlis, Malek, Melli, Riza Abbasi), Tashkent, Dushanbe, St Petersburg, Lahore, Cairo and Istanbul (TİEM, University Library). Cards whose shelfmark already appeared in a CUDL row were skipped.

16 rows marked Supplement were checked against the holding institution’s catalogue or a scholarly source (linked in the row). The six Princeton rows come from Princeton University Library MARC records. Central Asian additions and corrections follow Comstock-Skipp, Scions of Turan (Leiden, 2023); Topkapı notes follow Tanındı and Schmidt in Shahnama Studies II (2012).

Coverage

The old database recorded about 20,300 illustrations from 1,558 manuscripts across all periods (REF impact case study). Only about 845 manuscript cards survive in the web archive, so some holdings are still missing. It is a working corpus, not a census.

How fields were derived

  • Date, folios, institution, scribe, bibliography: copied from each record.
  • Region: normalized from the recorded place and date (e.g. Shiraz before 1453 = Timurid; 1453–1503 = Turkman). Records without a place are marked Uncertain.
  • Patron: only when the record names one. Blank means not stated, not “no patron”.
  • Paintings: the bold figure is a total stated in the record text; the muted figure counts narrative paintings catalogued in the Shahnama Project record, which may be partial.

Limits

  • Text dates and painting dates can differ (e.g. Princeton Garrett 58G: text 1674, miniatures likely late 18th century).
  • Dispersed manuscripts appear once per holding; e.g. the Shah Tahmasp and “Big Head” Shahnamas have several fragment rows.
  • Region and patron fields were extracted with AI help from catalogue prose (CUDL rows) or derived by rule from place and date (SnP archive rows). Check them against the linked record before citing.
  • c.1390–1400 (borderline) holds copies dated just before 1400, e.g. Cairo Dar al-Kutub Ta’rikh Farisi 73 (1393–94). Undetermined means the record gives no date or only a span wider than a century.
  • 40 rows are known only from auction sales; their current location is not stated.
  • Archived SnP cards date from 2010–18. Some are flagged by the project itself as incomplete or unverified (noted in the row).
